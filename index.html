The Future of Ethereum is Layer 2

Layer 2 networks make Ethereum faster and cheaper by moving most activity off the main chain, then posting proof back to Ethereum. Arbitrum is one of the leading Layer 2 rollups, giving builders low fees while keeping Ethereum's security model close by.

Think of it as an express lane for Ethereum apps: users get smoother transactions, and the mainnet still acts as the settlement layer.

Why Layer 2?

The best scaling tools keep Ethereum useful without asking users to wait or overpay for every transaction.

🐢

Ethereum's Bottleneck

Ethereum mainnet is secure and decentralized, but demand can make fees expensive. When the network is busy, simple transactions can become slow or costly.

⚡

What is Arbitrum?

Arbitrum is a Layer 2 rollup that batches many transactions off-chain. It then posts compressed transaction data back to Ethereum for settlement.

💸

Real-World Benefit

Users can see dramatically cheaper fees and faster app interactions. Builders can still inherit Ethereum-style security guarantees.
